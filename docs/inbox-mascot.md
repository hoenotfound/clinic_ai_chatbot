# DA Inbox mascot

The mascot uses a small transparent WebP cropped from the supplied artwork. CSS clips the same image into head, hands, rings and accents. It floats and waves in the conversation-list header, and responds only to identified message or failed-delivery events in the currently open conversation. No claim is made about actual AI thinking or booking confirmation.

Click the mascot to hide it; click Mascot off to restore it. The preference is browser-local (not account-wide), and reduced-motion settings disable animation. Background tabs pause animation. No new network polling, server events, database calls, Gemini calls or animation dependency.

Manual verification: open Inbox on desktop and mobile; check layout and dark mode, hover to wave, toggle off/on and reload, verify reduced motion, receive a message or failed delivery on the open thread, and confirm unrelated conversation changes do not trigger a reaction. Verify that all existing controls remain clickable.

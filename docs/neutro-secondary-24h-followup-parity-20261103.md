# Neutro secondary WhatsApp: 24h service-followup eligibility parity

## Clinic decision (3 Nov 2026)
For **2h, 6h and 20h customer-service follow-ups only**, use the same
customer-initiated, sender-bound 24-hour reply window as the primary number.
A separate, treatment-specific MARKETING consent record is no longer required
for the secondary's **new, post-cutover in-window** service follow-ups.

The client has NOT authorized unsolicited marketing messages. All marketing
opt-outs, business-wide STOPs, consent revocations, appointment and staff
handoff gates remain in force.

### What is explicitly unchanged
- 48h/66h and Day4/Day6 MARKETING templates require valid permission and
  Meta-approved templates on the correct WABA, including the current
  treatment-specific message-linked opt-in evidence. Keep billing checks.
- The original, already-approved 2h/6h/20h sequences for opted-in ad leads
  continue without having their starting time or previous one-shot slots reset.
- No historical synced contact is sent a reminder based on old messages.
- No new message is generated by deployment; only normal due-time worker scans
  select later customer-initiated conversations under unchanged rate limits.
- Before & After media and AI price images remain independently governed.
- All new or existing opt-outs, negative latest-lead consent, staff attention,
  confirmed booking, newer customer/staff messages, quiet hours, prior failures,
  and the 24h Meta service window continue to block service follow-ups.
- Primary sender behaviour, configuration and database rows are not modified.

### Release method
1. Merge CI-tested code and deploy while
   `WHATSAPP_SECONDARY_SERVICE_FOLLOWUP_PARITY_ACTIVATED_AT` is **absent**.
   The prior opted-in-only rule is then unchanged.
2. Once the code is live, set the flag to a **new UTC timestamp in the future**
   (no earlier than `WHATSAPP_SECONDARY_ALL_AUTOMATION_ACTIVATED_AT`).
3. After activation, only **fresh** secondary customer messages with a genuine
   sender-bound provider message ID and source timestamp may start the newly
   permitted service follow-ups. The customer must be in AI mode, without
   staff takeover or outstanding attention.
4. Verify candidates/attempts and provider receipts against real traffic;
   do not send a test to an existing customer or replay inbound messages.

### Rollback
Unset `WHATSAPP_SECONDARY_SERVICE_FOLLOWUP_PARITY_ACTIVATED_AT` on Neutro's
Render service. Older opted-in sequences remain eligible; newly included
nonconsenting service follow-ups stop immediately. No database data change or
customer-message retry is needed.

### Policy boundary
Meta's September 2026 messaging policy allows freeform service-window replies
for 24 hours after each incoming customer message. It still requires proper
recipient permission, category-appropriate messaging and honoring STOP/opt-out.
This code change is **not** permission to send marketing templates without a
documented opt-in or to continue after the 24-hour window.
